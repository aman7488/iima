# Asian Paints - MKT project

## Summary
### Flow Summary
Asian Paints' edge comes from experience and strategy (backward integration, painter CSR training), not portfolio or loyalty schemes; near-zero awareness of new businesses (Bath Sense, Sleek, White Teak) is a key marketing gap.

### Product and Customer Lifetime Value
- Speaker 3 flagged a gap: homeowners hear nothing from Asian Paints for 5 to 7 years after painting.
- Abhishek Maurya said Asian Paints addresses this by expanding from paint to a full home solution across seven businesses.
    - Wallpapers (Nilaya, Sabyasachi), Bath Sense (bath), Sleek (kitchen), White Teak (lighting), WeatherShield (UPVC)
- Bundling drives cross-sell: Smart Care waterproofing plus paint gives a longer warranty; BHPS teams pitch across verticals.
- Store tiers (Easy Color, Color Idea, Beautiful Home) target different purchasing-power segments.

### Competition and Margins
- Portfolio is not the gap; the market is fragmented (Birla Opus, Nerolac, Nippon, local brands like Ram Ratan), squeezing margins.
- Raw material costs pressure PAT and working capital, pushing big players toward vertical integration.
- Asian Paints built its own cement plant for Neo Bharat latex cement paint instead of buying from the Birlas.
- Differentiators: customer experience enabling premium pricing, and strategic value-chain moves; trade schemes and foreign trips are table stakes.

### Painters and Loyalty
- Painters drive most paint choices; rewards are a price war, but Asian Paints' 80+ year history builds trust.
- MasterStroke loyalty app is standard industry practice, not a differentiator.
- Real differentiator: Asian Paints Color Academy CSR, low-cost painter skill training with certification, building goodwill.
- Painters value respect and gifts like brushes and spatulas alongside money.

### Tinting Machines and Retailer Malpractice
- Other brands' colorants physically mix, but shades won't match Asian Paints G codes, risking customer complaints.
- Mixing brands also hurts retailer ROI since base and colorant carry separate MRPs and invoices.
- Some retailers mix Tractor emulsion with Damp Proof to imitate Royal and repackage it, cannibalizing Royal; such retailers were blacklisted.
    - Tiers: Tractor (economy), Apex/Accolite (mid), Royal (luxury)

### Promotion and New Business Awareness
- Speaker 3 noted mass media (TV, newspapers) isn't targeted at people about to repaint.
- ATL includes BCCI color partnership; BTL includes small email and cold-calling efforts, shop training, retail upgrades.
- Retailer schemes split by business value: 0 to 75 lakh retailers get higher per-liter incentives than large ones.
- Brand awareness for Bath Sense, Sleek, White Teak is near zero, hurting PAT; Speaker 3 will build on this for the project.
